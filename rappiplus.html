DATA ANALYTICS · EDUCATIONAL CASE STUDY

RappiPlus: Business Performance & Profitability

A multi-stage analytics project focused on understanding commercial performance, profitability, the customer purchase journey, retention, and experimental evaluation.

Python Pandas SQL Statistics Power BI DAX
Public portfolio version

This case study presents the analytical methodology, business questions, and Power BI report architecture. The Python notebook is publicly available on GitHub. Original datasets and Power BI files are not included in the repository.

Business questions

How can a business monitor commercial performance, compare product-level profitability, identify friction in its purchase funnel, understand customer retention, and evaluate whether an interface change affects conversion?

My analytical approach

01

Prepare the data

Review data quality, clean and transform records, and organize information for subsequent analyses using Python and Pandas.

02

Evaluate commercial performance

Calculate and compare revenue, available cost components, profitability indicators, and product-level performance.

03

Study customer behavior

Use SQL to analyze the conversion funnel and customer retention by cohorts.

04

Assess an experiment

Apply statistical hypothesis testing to an A/B experiment, distinguishing observed differences from statistically supported conclusions.

05

Communicate insights

Build a Power BI report with KPIs, temporal trends, product comparisons, filters, and detailed transaction navigation.

Dashboard architecture

The Power BI report was organized into three complementary views. The cards below are schematic descriptions, not screenshots or reproductions of the underlying business data.

01 / OVERVIEW

Executive overview

Summary KPIs, revenue evolution, accumulated performance, and product category comparisons.

02 / PRODUCTS

Product performance

Product-level revenue, costs, gross profit, units sold, and comparison across time.

03 / DETAILS

Transaction details

Drill-through navigation to inspect records associated with a selected product.

Analytical takeaways

The project demonstrates how commercial metrics, customer behavior analysis, statistical experimentation, and dashboard design can be combined in one analytical workflow.

  • Distinguish gross product profit from measures that additionally deduct marketing expenditure.
  • Compare product performance using both sales volume and profitability rather than revenue alone.
  • Locate funnel stages that merit further investigation, without assuming causation from descriptive results.
  • Interpret A/B tests using statistical evidence instead of treating a numerical difference as proof of improvement.
  • Use interactive reporting to connect executive indicators with more detailed product information.

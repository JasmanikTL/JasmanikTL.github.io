Grupo Andes: Real Estate Commercial Analytics

A Power BI business intelligence project to examine property sales, revenue performance, customer segments, sales channels, and repeat-purchase patterns.

Power BI DAX Star Schema Business KPIs Cohort Analysis
Public portfolio version

This case study presents the analytical methodology, business questions, dimensional model, and Power BI dashboard architecture. The project documentation is publicly available on GitHub. Original datasets, Power BI files, and report screenshots are not included in the public repository. The cover image is a conceptual illustration.

Business challenge

How can a real estate business evaluate its commercial performance across property types, cities, channels, and customer segments while identifying patterns in repeat purchases?

Analytical approach

01

Data preparation

Review transaction, customer, property, and channel fields for completeness, consistency, and appropriate data types.

02

Dimensional modeling

Organize property sales as a fact table linked to customer, property, channel, and date dimensions using a star-schema approach.

03

Business measures

Define revenue, property sales, average transaction value, commissions, and year-over-year growth measures in Power BI.

04

Segmentation and retention

Compare sales by city, property type, acquisition channel, and customer segment; examine subsequent purchases through cohort analysis.

05

Dashboard storytelling

Build a multi-page report that connects executive KPIs with segment-level comparisons and customer retention patterns.

Business insights and decisions

The analysis was designed to support differentiated commercial strategies rather than evaluating sales using a single aggregate measure.

  • Compare property categories by both transaction volume and average transaction value.
  • Assess geographic concentration of revenue and the contribution of each sales channel.
  • Distinguish first-time customers from returning buyers when planning retention initiatives.
  • Use year-over-year comparisons alongside monthly patterns to understand growth and variability.
  • Translate dashboard findings into channel, product mix, and customer relationship recommendations.

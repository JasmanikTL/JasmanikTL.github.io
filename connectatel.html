ConnectaTel: Customer Behavior & Segmentation

Exploratory analysis of telecommunications customer profiles and service usage, with a focus on data quality, behavioral segments, and business recommendations.

Portfolio presentation

This page summarizes the analytical workflow and qualitative findings documented in the project notebook. The complete analysis notebook and supporting datasets are available in the GitHub repository.. The cover is conceptual artwork, not a screenshot of customer records.

Business challenge

How can a telecommunications provider better understand customers' calling and messaging behavior, identify distinct usage profiles, and develop more relevant service offerings?

Analytical approach

01

Explore the source data

Inspect three source datasets, their schemas, missing values, and field types before analysis.

02

Resolve data-quality issues

Handle invalid age placeholders, unknown city values, out-of-range registration dates, and context-dependent missing values in activity records.

03

Build usage profiles

Aggregate service activity by user and summarize calling, messaging, and customer characteristics.

04

Explore distributions

Use descriptive statistics and visualizations to examine usage distributions, outliers, and customer age groups.

05

Segment and interpret

Classify customers into low-, medium-, and high-usage groups and translate observed patterns into commercial recommendations.

Key findings

  • Medium-usage customers form the largest behavioral segment, while high-usage customers are a smaller group with comparatively intensive activity.
  • Calls, messages, and minutes show right-skewed distributions, indicating that a limited number of users account for high consumption.
  • Adults represent the largest age segment in the project's analysis; the notebook reports no major age-distribution differences between Basic and Premium plans.
  • Missing churn dates were treated as meaningful non-cancellation records, rather than automatically filled or deleted.
Interpretation

These qualitative findings are taken from the notebook's executive analysis. No public dashboard or original customer records are embedded in this page.

Business recommendations

  • Explore tailored packages with more minutes and messages for high-usage customers.
  • Test lower-cost or promotional offers for low-usage customers.
  • Prioritize the medium-usage segment when designing the core service offering.
  • Monitor changes in usage behavior before proposing upsell campaigns or retention interventions.
